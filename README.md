# Swiggy Food for Thought Challenge 🍔
### Growth Strategy | Customer Acquisition | Retention Marketing

## 📌 Project Overview
This project explores growth opportunities in the food delivery market by analysing student preferences, identifying customer acquisition challenges, and proposing strategies to improve engagement and retention.

## 🎯 Objectives
- Understand student food ordering preferences and behaviour.
- Identify opportunities for customer acquisition and retention.
- Develop strategies to increase engagement and encourage repeat orders.

## 💡 Proposed Growth Strategy: Campus Credits
A gamified loyalty program designed to encourage students to order more frequently through:

- **Referral Rewards:** Incentivise users to invite friends.
- **Group Ordering:** Encourage collaborative orders among students.
- **Leaderboards:** Introduce friendly competition to increase engagement.
- **Squad Bonuses:** Reward groups for achieving shared ordering milestones.

## 📈 Go-to-Market Strategy
- Explore partnerships with college campuses.
- Use student-focused engagement and referral campaigns.
- Explore expansion opportunities in Tier 2 and Tier 3 markets.

## 🔍 Key Learnings
- Identifying customer acquisition and retention opportunities.
- Developing customer-centric growth strategies.
- Applying market research to business problem-solving.
- Designing gamification and referral-based engagement initiatives.

## 🛠️ Skills Demonstrated
Growth Strategy · Market Research · Customer Acquisition · Customer Retention · Go-to-Market Strategy · Problem-Solving

## ⚠️ Project Note
The strategies presented are proposals developed as part of the challenge. The projected outcomes are estimates, not verified business results.
